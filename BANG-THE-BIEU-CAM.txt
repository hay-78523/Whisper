BANG THE BIEU CAM - GIONG CLONE (viet the tieng Viet HOAC tieng Anh deu duoc)
==========================================================================
Dat the TRUOC cau. The tong giong / hieu ung / toc do GIU NGUYEN cho toi khi
gap the khac. [binh thuong] / [normal] = ve giong goc, tat het hieu ung.
LUU Y: cac tong giong GIU NGUYEN giong nhan vat. Giong co "mau cam xuc" (ray, va
giong them bang file dai 1-3 phut - /admin ghi "cam xuc: hao hung / tram") se doc
cau vui/hao hung bang doan GIONG THAT dang soi noi, cau buon/diu dang bang doan
giong that dang tram -> bieu cam ro. Giong chi co mau ngan: doi nhe toc do, am
luong, dau cau, ngat nghi. Rieng [phan dien] [tre con] [gia] co y DOI GIONG.
MEO DOC TU NHIEN NHAT:
 - Khong gan the cung duoc: giong co nhieu mau (ray) tu xoay vong mau de doc
   bot don dieu. Xuong dong trong (de 1 dong trong) giua cac doan = ngat chuyen y.
 - Chi dat the khi TONG GIONG DOI (1 the cho ca doan), dung dat the tung cau
   hay giua cau - cat vun thanh nhieu doan ngan lam giong doc roi rac, de loi.
